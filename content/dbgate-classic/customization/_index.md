Adapt DbGate to your environment and taste. You can restyle the interface with built-in or custom themes (including an AI theme assistant), and - for the web and self-hosted editions - control almost every aspect of runtime behavior through environment variables, from storage and authentication to logging and per-connection settings. Ready-made configuration samples cover the most common deployment scenarios.

* [Custom themes](custom-themes) - build and share your own color themes
* [Environment variables](env-variables) - configure storage, authentication, logging, connections and more
* [Web app config samples](web-app-config) - example configurations for common deployment scenarios
